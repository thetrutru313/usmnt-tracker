description: Product decisions behind permanent discovery exclusions and the scope of related news cleanup.

Noahkai Banks is intentionally excluded from the tracked pool following the
user's decision that he committed to Germany. His manually overridden,
dismissed candidate is a rediscovery block, not an abandoned review item.

**Why:** Removing only the tracked player would let discovery add him again;
a dismissal without a manual override can be revived by stored-signal rescoring.

**How to apply:** Do not remove this blocking candidate as routine cleanup or
reintroduce him through seeds without a new user decision.

For this removal, delete articles where he was the only tagged player. Keep
shared articles and remove only his tag. Untagged historical articles mentioning
his name are not deleted.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose to preserve other players' news and untagged
historical text rather than remove every textual mention.

**How to apply:** Verify structured player references separately from retained
article text. Possible RSS reingestion was accepted as a reporting concern, not
authorization to change ingestion or add a URL exclusion.
