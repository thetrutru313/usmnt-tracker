---
name: National-team row design
description: Product-owner decision on compact mobile match rows and preserving approved desktop presentation
---

Phone match rows should put date/status and broadcast on a meta line, then mirrored home/centre/away teams across the full row width. Do not reintroduce stacked team lines with separate far-right scores.

**Why:** The product owner explicitly rejected that stacked layout because it left an empty middle gap and separated the date from the teams. The desktop mirrored presentation was explicitly approved.

**How to apply:** For future responsive row changes, preserve the approved desktop appearance unless requested otherwise. Validate full team-name visibility at 375px, centre alignment, complete broadcasts and the desktop screenshot comparison.
