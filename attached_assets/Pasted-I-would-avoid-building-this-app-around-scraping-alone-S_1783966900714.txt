I would avoid building this app around scraping alone. Sports websites frequently change their HTML, many prohibit scraping in their Terms of Service, and you'll spend more time fixing scrapers than building features. A hybrid approach—official APIs + RSS feeds + a small amount of scraping where permitted—is much more reliable.

## My recommendation

If you're willing to spend around **$30–$100/month**, use **Sportmonks as your primary data provider** and supplement it with RSS feeds from U.S. Soccer, MLS, ESPN, BBC Sport, and official club sites. Reserve scraping for information that isn't available through APIs or RSS (such as certain Transfermarkt pages), and only where permitted. This approach will give you a far more reliable application than relying on scraping dozens of websites. ([Sportmonks][1])
