# Product Specification: USMNT Tracker

## Overview

Build a modern web application called **USMNT Tracker**.

The purpose of the application is to become the single source of truth for following United States Men's National Team players around the world.

The application should automatically aggregate news, fixtures, statistics, injuries, transfers, and player development information from multiple sources and present it in a clean dashboard.

This should feel similar to a personalized version of FotMob, Transfermarkt, and ESPN, but focused entirely on the USMNT player pool.

The application should require minimal manual updating.

---

# Core Goals

The app should answer questions like:

* Which USMNT players play today?
* Where can I watch them?
* Who is injured?
* Who transferred clubs?
* Who is playing well?
* Which prospects are breaking through?
* Who may receive a national team call-up soon?

---

# Users

Primary user:

A dedicated USMNT fan who follows players across dozens of clubs and leagues worldwide.

# Engineering Principles

* Modular architecture
* TypeScript throughout
* Server-side rendering where appropriate
* Clean API abstraction layer so data sources can be swapped easily
* Comprehensive error handling and logging
* Respect robots.txt and website terms of service; prefer official APIs and RSS feeds over scraping
* Background jobs should be idempotent and retry-safe
* Cache external API responses to reduce rate limits and costs

**Success Criteria:** The app should provide a near real-time, comprehensive view of the entire USMNT player pool—from established stars to emerging prospects—by automatically collecting and presenting relevant news, fixtures, statistics, injuries, transfers, and development updates in a fast, intuitive interface that requires little to no manual maintenance.
