### Recommended data model

Rather than assigning a streaming service to the league, model it at the **home club** level:

```json
{
  "league": "Liga MX",
  "homeClub": "Club América",
  "streaming": [
    "ViX"
  ],
  "tv": [
    "TUDN",
    "Univision"
  ]
}
```

This approach works because:

* América vs. Chivas (at América) → **ViX**
* Chivas vs. América (at Chivas) → **Peacock**
* Santos vs. América (at Santos) → **Fubo**
* América vs. Santos (at América) → **ViX**

The streaming service changes based solely on the **home team**, making `homeClub → streamingProvider` the correct relationship for fixture lookup. ([World Soccer Talk][1])

For a USMNT-focused app, this is also future-proof: if a player transfers to a Liga MX club, you only need to map them to their club, and every home fixture automatically inherits the correct streaming provider.
