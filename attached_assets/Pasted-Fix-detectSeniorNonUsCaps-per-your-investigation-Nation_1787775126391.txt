## The discriminator

API-Football's /teams?id={team.id} response includes a `national` boolean on
the team object. That is authoritative — use it rather than a hand-maintained
list of national teams.

ever:

- New table `api_football_teams`: api_football_team_id (pk), name,
  country, is_national (boolean), fetched_at.
- On encountering an unknown team id, fetch /teams?id= and store the row.
- Never re-fetch a cached team. A club does not become a national team.

## The gate

is national. Concretely:

  isNationalTeamAppearance = teamIsNational(team.id)

competition types, but it must never be sufficient on its own. Delete any
path where a league-name match alone produces a cap count.

dual nationals, which is the safer error: a missed flag costs a second look,
